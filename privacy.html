Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Summary

Clip-Maschine runs only on the operator's own computer. It has no server, no user accounts, no tracking and no advertising. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

2. Data from TikTok

When a TikTok account is connected, the tool receives an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload). These are stored only in a local file on the operator's computer and are used only to upload drafts to that account.

3. Data from Twitch

The tool downloads publicly available clips and their public information (title, view count, channel name) from Twitch. Clips are processed locally and stored on the operator's computer.

4. Speech recognition

Subtitles are created locally on the operator's computer. No audio is sent to external services.

5. Data shared with TikTok

Only the finished video file is sent to TikTok, through the official TikTok Content Posting API. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to content on TikTok.

6. Deleting data

Revoke access in the TikTok app under "Settings → Security → Manage app permissions". Local tokens and clips can be deleted by the operator at any time. On request we delete all locally stored data about your account.
