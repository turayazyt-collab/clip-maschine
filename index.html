Clip-Maschine

A personal desktop tool that turns Twitch clips into vertical short videos and uploads them as drafts to the operator's own TikTok accounts.

How it works

  1. The tool finds popular clips from Twitch streamers who have allowed clipping of their content.
  2. It cuts them to 9:16, adds subtitles and credits the streamer.
  3. The operator reviews every clip before it is sent.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded via the official TikTok Content Posting API as drafts. The account owner finishes and publishes them in the TikTok app.

The tool runs only on the operator's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.
