1. What Clip-Maschine is

Clip-Maschine is a private desktop tool. It edits short clips from Twitch and uploads them as drafts to TikTok accounts that belong to the operator of the tool. It is not a public service.

2. Who may use it

Only the operator and the TikTok accounts the operator connects. By connecting a TikTok account you confirm that you own that account or are allowed to manage it.

3. Content and rights

Clips are only used from streamers who allow their content to be clipped and reposted. Every upload credits the original streamer. If you are a rights holder and want a clip removed, contact us (see below) and it will be removed.

4. TikTok rules

All uploads must follow the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Clips are sent as drafts. The account owner decides whether and how they are published.

The tool is provided "as is", without any warranty. We are not liable for lost data, failed uploads or actions taken by TikTok or Twitch.

6. Ending use

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time in your TikTok settings under "Security → Manage app permissions". Stored access tokens are then no longer valid.
